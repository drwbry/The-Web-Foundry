## Need clarification?
### Is GIB a separate system? Does it replace SAP?
No. GIB runs directly inside SAP ECC and SAP S/4HANA, with all data and execution remaining within your SAP system. GIB does not replace SAP planning logic, MRP, or production functionality. Instead, it improves how effectively your live SAP system supports daily supply chain operations.
### Is GIB another planning engine?
No. GIB does not introduce a separate planning engine or external data layer. Teams work directly with live SAP data, allowing planning and operational decisions to translate into faster, more coordinated execution.
### How is GIB different from standard SAP functionality?
GIB improves how teams interpret and act on live SAP data. It helps organizations respond faster to supply chain signals, reduce corrective adjustments, and keep execution aligned across planning, procurement, inventory, and production.
### Does GIB require heavy customization or modification of SAP?
No. GIB is purpose-built for SAP and integrates within standard ECC and S/4HANA environments. It consumes your existing master data, transactional data, and authorization concepts, allowing organizations to improve supply chain performance without disrupting core SAP functionality or clean core principles.
### Is GIB relevant if we already use SAP IBP or other planning tools?
Yes. GIB ensures that planning signals — whether from SAP IBP or a third-party solution — translate into faster, more coordinated execution inside SAP.
