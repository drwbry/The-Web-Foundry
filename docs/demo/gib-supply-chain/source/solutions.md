### Bring Execution Under Control in SAP
Planning systems generate signals. SAP executes them. Integrated Supply Chain Execution (ISCE) connects the two. It organizes planning signals - no matter what advanced planning system they come from - into priorities, connects related decisions, and brings planning and execution into one coordinated view within your live SAP system.
ISCE makes it clear what to do next and keeps decisions aligned across the supply chain.
