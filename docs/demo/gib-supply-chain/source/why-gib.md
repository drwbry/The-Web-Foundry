# Built-in Cross-Functional Intelligence for SAP Supply Chains
## Remove the friction that slows your supply chain down
### Decision Speed
#### Accelerate how work moves through SAP
##### Decision speed is not just about alerts. It’s about how quickly planners can evaluate information, coordinate across roles, and execute actions. GIB reduces friction in the decision process itself, improving responsiveness across the supply chain and enabling supply chain teams to move from analysis to execution faster inside SAP.
### Rework Rate
#### Increase first-time execution quality
##### Rework happens when operational decisions have to be adjusted after execution. Orders are changed, parameters are revised, or transactions are reversed. GIB improves the quality and completeness of information available at the moment a decision is made, so actions are correct the first time and require fewer downstream adjustments.
### Exception Cycle Time
#### Shorten the time to operational resolution
##### Operational issues like supply gaps, scheduling conflicts, and coverage risks are part of daily supply chain management. What matters is how quickly they are resolved. GIB enables teams to identify, prioritize, and address issues directly within SAP, reducing the time between detection and resolution.
### Workaround Frequency
#### Reduce reliance on parallel processes
##### When coordination requires exporting data, reconciling spreadsheets, or emailing across functions, execution slows and system integrity weakens. GIB keeps evaluation and action within SAP, reducing the need for external coordination layers.
