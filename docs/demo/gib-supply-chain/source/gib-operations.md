See All Relevant MRP Data in One Screen
[IMAGE: shots/gib-operations-dashboard-with-menu-options.png]
GIB consolidates stock, requirements, planned orders, purchase orders, and production orders into a single structured execution view. Instead of reviewing MD04 material by material, planners analyze demand and supply across multiple materials and plants in one list. Period-based columns and consumption history provide context for evaluating shortages and excess coverage across time.
Prioritize Exception Messages and Coverage Risk by Operational Impact
[IMAGE: shots/define-traffic-lights-gib-operations.png]
Coverage is calculated based on current stock and incoming receipts. GIB shows how many days of demand are covered by stock alone, by the first planned receipt, and by the second planned receipt. If coverage falls below defined limits, the material is flagged in red or yellow. Planners can filter by plant, MRP controller, exception group, or vendor to focus on operational risk rather than scanning raw MRP output.
Monitor Supplier Performance and Delivery Risk
[IMAGE: shots/gib-operations-monitor-vendors-and-deliveries.png]
Supplier lead times are evaluated using actual goods receipt history. Delivery deviations between confirmed and actual dates are tracked and visualized. Recurring delays, irregular performance, and risk patterns are identified early, allowing planners to adjust replenishment strategies or initiate supplier discussions before shortages occur.
Identify and Manage Missing Parts Collectively
[IMAGE: shots/gib-operations-missing-parts.png]
GIB detects component shortages and flags materials at risk due to insufficient supply. Instead of discovering missing parts during order conversion or production release, planners can see cross-material shortages in one list. Drill-down navigation allows users to trace shortages to specific orders and take corrective action directly within SAP. This allows planners to initiate procurement or reschedule activities before production or delivery is disrupted.
Execute Planning and Procurement Actions Directly in SAP
[IMAGE: shots/gib-operations-po-conversion-and-sales-orders-delivery.png]
Purchase requisitions can be converted to purchase orders directly from GIB. Planners can navigate to related sales orders, production orders, or purchase documents without leaving the cockpit. Relevant master data fields can be reviewed and adjusted, and simulations can be performed without immediately triggering MRP changes. Operational action is embedded within the same interface used for analysis.
Coordinate Multi-Level and Project-Based Planning
[IMAGE: shots/gib-operations-project-png.png]
GIB links project level demand to all dependent materials within the BOM structure. Planners can review whether stock and confirmed receipts exist for every required component, not just the finished good. If a lower-level material is short, the shortage is visible before project execution starts. This prevents incomplete builds and improves coordination across procurement and production.
